ASTRAL and VOLT fire at 900 RPM within each burst. Their trigger cycles are 0.45s and 0.40s respectively. Switching, reloading, losing focus or pausing cancels pending attacks. Automatic weapons fire while left click is held. OMEGA requires 0.6s of continuous spin-up before consuming ammunition; releasing the trigger resets it.

NOVA moves 12% faster than standard weapons. TITAN and RAILBREAKER move 15% slower, OMEGA 28% slower and HYPERION 7% slower. Recoil and accuracy differ per weapon. Snipers have a clear right-click scope and slower equip recovery. RAILBREAKER penetrates up to 0.35m of cover with 70% damage; thick cover blocks it. Thin cyan panels in every arena provide a penetration lane.

Shotgun pellets spread and lose damage with distance: BREACH falls off beyond 5m to a 24m limit; EXECUTOR and RAVAGER fall off beyond 4m to 22m and 20m limits. Their crosshair is a dotted ring. Headshots normally deal 2× damage; RAILBREAKER uses 1.5×. Limbs take 0.75× and feet 0.5×. HUD hit values show actual health removed, so a lethal hit may display less than the weapon's full damage.

Models have animated energy cells, bolts, pump and break-action mechanisms, rotary barrels, capacitors, impact cores and dual blades. Empty firearms reload automatically. Reload progress, spin-up and heavy wind-up appear on the HUD. Character ultimates apply to the selected arsenal: ION supplies unlimited ammunition for ten seconds and RAZE increases attack speed and damage by 50%.

Assault rifles (VXR-9 and ASTRAL-7), SMGs (NOVA-9 and STRIKER-11), and LMGs (TITAN-60 and OMEGA-88) use holographic versions of their mythic-style sights: PHASE LANCE rifle fins, compact VOID TALON SMG wings and reinforced SENTINEL CROWN LMG rails. Translucent projected fins, scan lines and luminous contours frame a tiny camera-aligned aiming zero. The projection appears only during aim; hip view shows the compact emitter mount. Release, reload, switching or losing capture hides it. There is no glass lens, top frame, circle-dot ring, bracket reticle or text beneath the aiming point. STRIKER uses violet energy, NOVA mint, PHANTOM ice cyan, ASTRAL lavender, TITAN amber and OMEGA coral. Aim retains the previous category FOVs and hold/tap bindings. Firing, recoil, spread, damage and spin-up are unchanged in training and multiplayer; sniper precision optics remain unchanged. The loadout preview displays the active hologram for inspection.

All fourteen firearms have a forged chassis with beveled armor, recessed ejection/chamber details, fasteners, grip ribs, trigger guard, serial stencils and machined muzzle bores. Long guns have ventilated barrel shrouds; LMGs have visible feed hardware, pistols serrated slides, and SMGs compact lower receivers. Armory previews show the complete stock in a rotating side view; first-person models omit the shoulder stock so it does not clip through the camera. Existing bolt, cell/drum, pump, hinge, rotor, reload and inspection animations still drive their respective parts. Native Three.js geometry and materials are used without external model downloads.
Low graphics uses simpler diffuse armor shading and fewer decorative vents, screws and glow layers. Medium/High retain the metallic finish, serial stencils and local weapon lighting; the iron-sight zero and gameplay rules stay identical.
